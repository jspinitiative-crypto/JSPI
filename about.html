Our mission

Make the path
easier to understand.

JSPI helps learners discover, prepare for, and pursue a surgical career through structured education and progressive opportunities.

Surgical interests often develop before a student has a clear picture of the route ahead. Career guidance, practical learning, research experience, and mentorship can feel disconnected. JSPI brings these elements into a framework that makes the next step easier to identify.

Our vision is a surgical education community in which a learner’s access to guidance and preparation is less dependent on where they study or whom they already know.

Our educational approach

A useful introduction.
A practical next step.
A pathway that grows.

Surgical Day is the common starting point. Every edition brings together four lectures covering Knowledge, Technical Skills, Research, and Future of Surgery. Topics change, while the educational structure stays consistent.

The wider pathway supports continued development through clinical exposure, professionalism and leadership, wellbeing, mentorship, and focused learning opportunities.

Explorer, Builder, Candidate, and Applicant describe developmental stages rather than fixed categories. Participants can use the framework according to their interests, experience, and training context.

The principles behind the program

Useful learning.
Responsible development.

01

Clarity

Every activity should have a clear purpose and an understandable learning outcome. Guidance should help learners make decisions, with official requirements checked at their source.

02

Access

The framework welcomes learners across institutions and stages of training. Inclusive participation and attention to practical barriers guide how opportunities are designed.

03

Educational quality

Practical learning needs appropriate supervision and feedback. Research needs sound methods, ethical review where required, and honest recognition of contributions.

04

Progress

Attendance is a beginning. Skills development, reflection, academic contribution, and teaching help show what a participant has learned and how they are moving forward.

05

Sustainability

Realistic workloads, peer support, documented processes, and continuity in educational roles help the initiative grow without depending on a single cohort.

06

Shared responsibility

Students, residents, surgeons, and educators each bring a useful perspective. Collaboration should have clear roles, appropriate oversight, and a shared educational purpose.

Residents & surgical trainees

Surgeons & academic educators

Contribute educational expertise through teaching, supervision, mentorship, and program feedback.

Institutions & educational partners

Support structured learning through faculty contributions, appropriate facilities, collaborative programs, and evaluation.
