  • Observership places
  • Venues and faculty time

You get

  • Partner recognition

Medical schools & faculties

You give

  • Skills labs and lecture halls
  • Reach to students

You get

  • A structured extracurricular pathway
  • Outcome data on your students
  • Co-branded activities

Student societies

You give

  • Reach and volunteers
  • Campus chapter leads

You get

  • Official JSPI chapter status
  • Shared events

Sponsors

  • Funding, skills kits, prizes
  • Fee waivers for learners

You get

  • Credible early access to future surgeons
  • Measurable visibility
  • Named activities

Our standards

Safe to partner with.

  • Education firstEvery activity has a learning outcome.
  • Host approvalEach placement follows host rules and supervision.
  • Fair accessPlaces are matched on merit, with waivers for need.
