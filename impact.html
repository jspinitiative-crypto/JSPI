Impact

What we measure,
and how we report it.

Every activity is counted, verified and reported openly.

What we measure

Four kinds of impact.

Reach

  • Learners by stage and university
  • Activities held
  • Fee waivers granted

Skills

  • Skills checks passed
  • Tiers reached
  • Teaching sessions by members

Academic output

  • Abstracts and posters
  • Papers submitted
  • Research teams active

Careers

  • Observerships completed
  • Residency destinations, tracked yearly
  • Mentor matches

How we report

Open numbers.

  1. After every activityAttendance, skills checks and a rating
  2. Every quarterKey figures are updated here
  3. Every yearA public impact report, including residency outcomes
