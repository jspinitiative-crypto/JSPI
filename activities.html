Questions

Before you join.

Who can join?

Medical students at any stage, interns and residents. Each activity shows the stages it is designed for.

Do activities cost anything?

Some are free and some carry a modest fee. Discounts and needs-based waivers are reserved for every activity.

How do points get recorded?

A tutor, mentor or supervisor verifies your participation, and the points are added to your Surgical Passport.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Verified participation is recorded, and Surgical Day and Skills Lab attendees receive a certificate.
