A starting point with substance

See the breadth.
Find your direction.

A surgical career calls for more than one kind of preparation. Surgical Day puts the essential perspectives in the same room.

A lecture from each domain connects scientific understanding, practical technique, research, and the changing future of surgery. Each edition offers a coherent educational experience and introduces the wider opportunities within JSPI.

The series welcomes medical students at all stages, interns exploring their next step, and residents interested in learning and educational contribution. You can join with a defined surgical interest or simply a question about where to begin.

Surgical Day is the flagship series. Skills workshops, specialty events, and other JSPI activities have their own formats and are announced separately.

The educational framework

Four domains.
A connected view of surgery.

Explore the purpose of each lecture domain. These topics illustrate the scope of the series; each edition has its own announced program.

Knowledge

Understand the foundations.

Build the scientific understanding that makes surgical practice meaningful. This domain connects anatomy and physiology with clinical reasoning, perioperative care, and the principles behind an operation.

Illustrative topics

  • Applied surgical anatomy and physiology
  • Surgical principles and clinical reasoning
  • Perioperative care and patient safety

Learning focus: Recognize how foundational science informs surgical decisions, and identify the concepts you need to strengthen at your stage of training.

Technical Skills

Turn understanding into practice.

Understand the techniques, habits, and preparation behind safe practical work. Surgical Day introduces core skills and explains how deliberate practice and constructive feedback support their development.

Illustrative topics

  • Suturing and knot-tying principles
  • Sterile technique and theatre fundamentals
  • Basic procedures and simulation-based learning

Learning focus: Understand the purpose and limits of foundational techniques, and plan the next step in supervised skills development.

Research

Ask better questions.

Learn how surgical questions become research, and how evidence informs practice. The focus is on reading critically, understanding study design, and finding a responsible starting point for academic work.

Illustrative topics

  • Finding and evaluating surgical literature
  • Study design, methodology, and statistics
  • Scientific writing and research collaboration

Learning focus: Frame a focused research question, recognize basic study designs, and identify an appropriate route into supervised academic work.

Future of Surgery

Prepare for a changing field.

Explore how surgical practice is changing and what those changes mean for the people who will deliver it. Technical innovation belongs alongside questions about evidence, ethics, access, and the future surgical workforce.

Illustrative topics

  • Robotic surgery and emerging technologies
  • Artificial intelligence and digital health
  • Simulation, innovation, and the surgical workforce

Learning focus: Discuss emerging developments thoughtfully, including their limitations, and identify the skills a changing surgical field may require.

Ask about Surgical Day

A few practical questions

Before you join.

Do I need to have chosen a surgical specialty?

No. The series is useful both for learners exploring surgery and those developing an existing interest. The four domains provide a shared starting point.

Are the lectures the same at every event?

The domains remain Knowledge, Technical Skills, Research, and Future of Surgery. Lecture topics change between editions, allowing the series to explore different questions and levels of interest.

Does Surgical Day include hands-on training?

The core format consists of four educational lectures. Practical workshops and supervised simulation are separate activities unless explicitly included in an edition’s published program.

How do I register or find event details?

Registration arrangements accompany each event announcement. Until an edition’s details are available, contact JSPI by email to express interest or ask a question.
