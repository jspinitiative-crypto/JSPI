Builder

Candidate

Bring your experience together into a portfolio.

Aim for Chief · 500 pts

Your checklist

  • Complete a merit-matched observership
  • Develop supervised research to submission
  • Keep a verified clinical and learning log
  • Plan exams, electives and your CV

Surgical Passport · Scalpel Points

Your progress, verified.

The Passport records every verified activity. The full points system opens with the Passport launch.

  1. Take partJoin any JSPI activity.
  2. Get verifiedA tutor, mentor or supervisor confirms it.
  3. Earn pointsScalpel Points add to your Passport.
  4. Unlock moreHigher tiers open mentors, observerships and teaching.
  1. 0 ptsObserver
  2. 100 ptsFirst Assistant
  3. 250 ptsOperator
  4. 500+ ptsChief

Points reflect activity within JSPI. They do not replace academic assessment or determine residency eligibility.
